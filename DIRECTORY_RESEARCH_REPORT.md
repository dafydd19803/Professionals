# Commercial Property Professionals Guide: directory research

Research date: 24 November 2026. The directory retains all 19 businesses from the supplied WordPress guide. Only claims supported by an identifiable official source have been added to the JSON. No third-party logos have been copied.

This report documents verification of the **original 19 WordPress listings**. Those records remain intact. Seventeen verified businesses researched for Stages 03–06 and the guide publisher’s Stage 04 capital allowances listing have since been added; the live directory now contains 37 records. See [`PROPOSED_DIRECTORY_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`](PROPOSED_DIRECTORY_RESEARCH_REPORT.md) for merged additions and the six businesses still awaiting approval.

## Verification summary

- **Verified (2):** Timothy A Brown; Williams Ellis.
- **Partially verified (5):** Savills; UMi; Cullimore Dutton; Oliver & Co; WR Partners.
- **Needs review (12):** Halls; Roger Parry & Partners; TSR; Bathgate; Banc; At Legal; Debsurveys; Local Architects Direct; Coxeys; Ellis & Co; The Uncommon Practice; RCH.

Web access allowed some official pages to be checked, but general search engines were challenged or returned irrelevant results and multiple candidate company domains were blocked, unavailable or ambiguous. Those cases are marked for manual research rather than filled from guesses.

The existing guide's Stage 1 areas for Halls, Roger Parry & Partners and TSR are retained in the JSON as user-supplied values, but could not be independently confirmed from an official site. The three regions for Savills are based on its official statement that its commercial teams are based all over the UK; the research did not confirm a local office in each region.
